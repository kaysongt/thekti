# Ministers Training Handbook

Working draft for KTI faculty review. Intended for ministers, ministry trainees, and the pastors who supervise them.

## Purpose and use

Use this handbook alongside supervised ministry practice. For each unit, study the suggested Scripture, discuss the case with a mentor, complete the exercise, and record the mentor’s feedback. Local church leaders should supply their approved procedures, reporting contacts, and role descriptions before the handbook is distributed.

## Units

| Unit | Scripture for study | Ministry practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calling, character, and accountability | 1 Timothy 3:1–13; 1 Peter 5:1–4 | Write a ministry role description and agree with a mentor on responsibilities, boundaries, and a review rhythm. |
| Prayer and spiritual formation | Acts 2:42; 1 Timothy 4:12–16 | Build a weekly rhythm of prayer, Scripture study, rest, and accountability. Review it after two weeks. |
| Teaching and preaching | 2 Timothy 2:15; 4:1–5 | Prepare and deliver a short teaching. Ask the mentor to review how it handles the biblical text and applies it to the audience. |
| Pastoral care and confidentiality | Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:1–2 | Role-play a care conversation. Practice explaining confidentiality limits and handing off a concern through the church’s approved procedure. |
| Discipleship and small groups | Matthew 28:18–20; 2 Timothy 2:2 | Lead a discussion, agree on a next step with participants, and practice an appropriate follow-up. |
| Evangelism and community outreach | Acts 1:8; Colossians 4:5–6 | Plan an outreach activity with a clear invitation, a follow-up owner, and a way to review what was learned. |
| Team leadership and administration | Acts 6:1–7; 1 Corinthians 14:40 | Create a volunteer roster and a service plan. Walk through how the team handles an absence, a conflict, and a handoff. |
| Stewardship and ministry ethics | 2 Corinthians 8:20–21; 1 Peter 4:10 | Review a fictional ministry expense and identify who approves, records, and reconciles it under the church’s financial procedures. |

## Supervised practice record

For each exercise, record the date, activity, mentor, feedback, and one next step. Use anonymized examples for pastoral care and incident scenarios. Keep personal records in the church’s approved system.

At the final review, the trainee and mentor discuss biblical handling, judgment, communication, reliability, and response to feedback. Record which responsibilities are ready for independent practice and which still need supervision.
